Commercial real estate, every number sourced.

Launching January 2027

One weekly briefing on commercial real estate across borders. Every figure links to where it came from.

Sourced CRE is the international edition of CRE Insider, the Italian weekly on commercial real estate. Same method, wider map: one analysis a week, readable in five minutes, written for investors, asset managers, lenders and the companies that serve them.

Most market commentary repeats numbers without telling you where they came from. We do the opposite. Every figure in Sourced CRE links to its primary source: a statistics office, a central bank, a land registry, a company filing. If we cannot source it, we do not publish it.

What we cover

  • United States. Office, industrial and capital markets, built on public data such as FRED and SEC filings.
  • Gulf. Dubai and the UAE, using official transaction data published by the Dubai Land Department.
  • Southern Europe. Spain and Italy, including cross-border capital flowing into the Mediterranean.

How we work

Each issue is researched with the help of AI tools and checked by the editor against the original sources before it goes out. We say so on every page, because readers have a right to know. Our editorial policy is published in full on the CRE Insider masthead page.

Who is behind it
